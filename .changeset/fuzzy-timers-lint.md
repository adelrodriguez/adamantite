Enable five new Oxlint rules across the core, React, and Vitest presets: `no-unreachable-loop`, `unicorn/explicit-timer-delay`, `unicorn/no-confusing-array-with`, `react/function-component-definition`, and `vitest/padding-around-test-blocks`. The existing `no-constant-binary-expression` rule also gains Oxlint's new relational-comparison diagnostics.
